# Homepage (app/page.tsx)

Scope: the homepage of karimbabasf.com, plus the resume page chrome, favicon and share image, which carry the same world. Visitor mode: Persuade. Success is a project opened, the resume read, or an email sent.

Audience and job: founders, hiring teams and fellowship reviewers opening a link from X, LinkedIn or an intro, often on a phone, deciding in under a minute. Proof is the four shipped projects, each shown doing its job, and the craft of the page itself. Constraints: Karim's reading order (picture, name, about, projects, resume and email, then X, LinkedIn, GitHub); portrait small and in colour; his 2026-10-01 brief pins professional, simple, motion design, depth, high quality type. Build path: code-led (no harness image tool; the API fallback bills his key).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is one precision instrument milled from aluminium by one person: an engraved nameplate, a small glass window holding his photo, and four project modules whose glass displays run each product's mechanism. It refuses the dark dev portfolio (hero, project grid, stack pills), the SaaS bento landing page, and the bare minimal text column.

OWN-WORLD: Bead-blasted silver aluminium under one directional light that follows the pointer (scroll on touch); chamfers catch it and shadows swing a few pixels with it. Type is paint-filled engraving in near-black. Smoked glass displays sit recessed in the face, readouts in a code face, one cool display ink. One fixed ramp of aluminium tones, nothing between its steps. Amber is the only warm light and only ever means "waits for a person". Google Sans Flex (extended width for engraving, text width for reading) with Google Sans Code for readouts.

STORY: The visitor sees a face in a glass window and a name engraved across the full measure, reads what he builds in two sentences, watches four displays run four mechanisms (a swap above the limit waiting for a click, a radar of agent sessions, agents bidding, two models and a policy then a person), presses one module to read how it works and follow its links, and ends at the I/O panel: Resume, Email with its amber lamp lit (it waits for a person), X, LinkedIn, GitHub.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 1440x900, one 880px column on a full-bleed aluminium face. Top row: the portrait window (about 96x120, recessed smoked glass, colour photo), then the name engraved in extended caps filling the remaining measure at up to 6rem, a San Francisco local time readout under it. Below: the lede at about 30px and the statement at body size. Then the recessed rack opens with Phosphor's module, its display already running, and Warden's at the fold. Light enters top left; the displays power on once, in order, down the page.

FORM: machined instrument (industrial design: milled aluminium, engraving, glass displays), position 5 of 7 on the ordered list (1 approval sheet, 2 wallet passes, 3 release notes, 4 optical glass, 5 machined instrument, 6 engineering datasheet, 7 security print). Seed key 4f01b8c1. Signature interaction: the light follows the pointer across every chamfer and engraving; pressing a module depresses it and opens its drawer on a spring. Raises from declined challengers: one power-on sequence down one axis, never per-section entrances (Versailles bosquet vistas); amber is the only warm light, and readout digits change as layered glass (nixie laboratory counter); the name is cut to fill its measure (Hatch Show print); a fixed tonal ramp is the only neutral palette (exposure record). Honest risk: skeuomorphic kitsch; held back by bead blast not brushed metal, one light, flat engraved type, and no screws, knobs or rack ears.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
