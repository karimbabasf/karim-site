# Homepage (app/page.tsx)

Scope: the homepage of karimbabasf.com. Visitor mode: Persuade. The visitor decides whether to reach out; success is a project opened, the resume read, or an email sent.

Audience and job: founders, hiring teams and fellowship reviewers opening a link from X, LinkedIn or an intro, often on a phone, deciding in under a minute. Proof is the four shipped projects and the craft of the page itself. Constraints: Karim's reading order (picture, name, about, projects, resume and email, then X, LinkedIn, GitHub); portrait small and in colour; arlan.me's quiet single column is the pinned vibe, "looks like it's written on paper" the pinned twist.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is Karim's own card file: one ruled index card with his name written in pen and his colour photo clipped to it, and the project cards filed under it, each pulled open to be read. It refuses the dark portfolio of hero, project grid and stack pills, and it refuses the full-bleed notebook page.

OWN-WORLD: White index card stock on a pale desk. Each card has one thin red header rule and pale blue rules on a fixed pitch that every typed baseline sits on. Typed ink is one sans at one size: black for facts, grey for kinds and dates. Blue ballpoint is Karim's hand only: the name and the signature, plotted from single-stroke paths. His photo is an instax print, the only full colour, held by a steel Gem clip. Soft offset paper shadows. No borders, pills, badges or icon tiles.

STORY: The visitor meets a person first (face, handwritten name), reads two typed sentences about what he builds, scans four project cards like a list, pulls one to read how it works, and ends at the front card: resume, email, three profiles, signed.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 1440x900, one centred 600px column. Top: the identity card, about 600x380, turned -0.6deg, the name written across its header band at about 40px cap height, stroke by stroke on load; the instax print, 104px wide and turned +4deg, clipped over the top right corner, half above the edge. Lede and statement typed on the rules. 40px below: the file, four card strips (name left, kind right, one-liner on the first rule), each tucked under the next. The front card with Resume and Email starts at the fold.

FORM: index card file, position 7 of 7 on the ordered list (1 ruled notebook page, 2 engineering lab notebook, 3 legal pad, 4 thermal receipt, 5 typed letter, 6 carbon approval form, 7 index card file). Seed key 4dc3737f. Signature interaction: pulling a card, the cards in front of it part on a soft spring; on hover they part a few pixels as a hint. Focal motion: the pen writes the name once on load. Raises kept from the declined hand: one scale jump, the handwritten name (from the Greiman collage); an ink law, each ink one job (from the CRT arcade); one typed size, hierarchy by indent, weight and rules only (from the phosphor terminal); handwriting drawn from stroke data, never a script font (from the jacquard brocade). Honest risk: clip, print and cards can tip into scrapbook kitsch; held back by one typed size, faint rules and three handwritten marks at most.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
