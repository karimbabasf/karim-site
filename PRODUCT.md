# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
People who meet Karim Baba (an engineer and founder in San Francisco) through his card, his X posts or an intro, and open his site to judge whether he is worth following, hiring or backing. On the blog, they arrive from a link to one post. (Inferred from the site and the owner's notes; not confirmed in an interview, because the owner works in no-questions mode.)

## Product Purpose
karimbabasf.com is Karim's personal site: who he is, what he built, his resume. The blog (blog.karimbabasf.com) holds his long-form writing. Success: a reader finishes the post, then follows him on X or emails him.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Next.js 16 app, deployed on Vercel; a push to main ships production.
- The homepage is a hand-built static page, public/home.html, with its own i18n (public/i18n.json, hashed address).
- Blog posts are MDX files in content/blog/. Sample posts carry `sample: true` and never render in production.

## Brand Commitments
- Black room, white light, Manrope, black and white only. The resume is a white sheet in the black room.
- No display serifs, no pills, no glow, no outlines on cards.

## Evidence on Hand
No real posts yet. Never write posts in Karim's voice: sample posts describe the layout only.

## Product Principles
- The writing is the product; chrome stays out of its way.
- One world across the site: a visitor should feel they never left karimbabasf.com.
- Every post ends in one clear next step: follow or write to him.
